Privatnost — DroneLogbook

Zadnja izmjena: studeni 2026.

Što aplikacija sprema

  • Pri prijavi Google računom: ime, e-adresu i profilnu sliku (Firebase Authentication).
  • Tvoje letove, održavanje letjelica, podatke o dozvolama i dokumentima te postavke (Google Firestore). Vidljivi su samo tebi.
  • Na uređaju (preglednik): lokalnu kopiju podataka radi rada bez interneta.

Što ne radimo

  • Ne prodajemo tvoje podatke. Pružatelji usluga navedeni u nastavku obrađuju ih samo koliko je potrebno za rad aplikacije; ne koristimo ih za oglašavanje.
  • Ne prikazujemo oglase i ne koristimo analitiku u aplikaciji.

Brisanje podataka

U aplikaciji, u Postavkama profila (ikona zupčanika) → „Opasna zona”, možeš trajno obrisati račun i sve svoje podatke u oblaku (letove, održavanja, dokumente, pravila i profil). Možeš i poslati zahtjev na drone@zrina.app.

Pružatelji usluga

Google Firebase (prijava i baza podataka) i Cloudflare (hosting i domena).

Kontakt

drone@zrina.app

Privacy — DroneLogbook

Last updated: November 2026

What the app stores

  • When you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture (Firebase Authentication).
  • Your flights, aircraft maintenance, licence and document data, and settings (Google Firestore). They are visible only to you.
  • On your device (browser): a local copy of your data so the app works offline.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data. The service providers listed below process it only as needed to operate the app; we do not use it for advertising.
  • We show no ads and use no analytics inside the app.

Deleting your data

In the app, under Profile settings (gear icon) → “Danger zone”, you can permanently delete your account and all your cloud data (flights, maintenance, documents, rules and profile). You can also email drone@zrina.app.

Service providers

Google Firebase (sign-in and database) and Cloudflare (hosting and domain).

Contact

drone@zrina.app
