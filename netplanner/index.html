How the planner counts addresses

IPv4 VLSM allocations reserve the network and broadcast addresses when sizing a subnet. IPv6 requests use the full address count. The cloud options subtract the documented count of reserved IPv4 addresses per subnet; they do not check provider quotas, routing limits, or other platform-specific rules.

Overlap, gap, and allocation results describe only the CIDRs entered here. They are planning calculations, not a check of live cloud or on-premises configuration.

All calculations stay in this browser unless you choose to copy a share link.
