Create QR codes in your own colors and shapes, add a logo, or read a code from an image or camera. Processing stays in your browser.

Scan to join network: point a phone camera at the code and tap the join prompt. Support varies by device; hidden networks and some older scanners may need manual setup.

    Style and export settings

    Presets change the colors and shape. Always test your finished code with several devices before printing.

    Print-ready uses whole-pixel modules, a solid background and a quiet margin of at least 4 modules. Leftover pixels are added to the margin so the image is exactly the size you chose, and 300 DPI is written into the PNG metadata. The physical size is pixels ÷ 300 (1024 px ≈ 8.67 cm). Very dense codes at small sizes cannot use whole pixels and ask for a larger size. Check the printed size and test-scan before mass printing.

    Privacy

    QR codes, logos, WiFi passwords, contact details, selected images and camera frames are processed in your browser. The camera starts only with your permission and stops when the page is hidden. Nothing is sent to a server and no analytics run on this tool. Content is not saved after you close the page.

    Good to know

    • A QR code is not encrypted: anyone who scans it reads its content, including WiFi passwords.
    • Use dark modules on a light background and keep a quiet margin. Long content creates denser codes that need a larger print size.
    • Adding a logo covers part of the code, so error correction is raised to H. Always test-scan the result.
    • Styled modules can be harder for some scanners to read. The positioning and other reserved patterns remain square; use the classic style if scanning fails.
