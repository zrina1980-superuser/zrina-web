DST can skip a local time in spring or repeat it in autumn. GitHub Actions schedules always use UTC; workflow starts may be delayed by up to 15 minutes.

Cron syntax

Linux, Kubernetes and GitHub Actions use five fields: minute, hour, day of month, month and day of week. EventBridge uses six: minute, hour, day of month, month, day of week and year; its day-of-month or day-of-week field must use ? when the other is specified. EventBridge numbers weekdays 1–7 (Sunday–Saturday), unlike POSIX 0–6 (Sunday–Saturday). Operators supported here: *, ,, - and /; month and weekday names are also accepted.

Day-of-month and day-of-week

When both fields are restricted (neither starts with *), standard cron runs the job when either matches. If one is *, only the other restricts the schedule.

Scope

This tool does not support Quartz seconds or special day operators such as L, W and #. Linux macros such as @monthly are not portable to Kubernetes, GitHub Actions or EventBridge; convert a regular expression instead. Kubernetes time zones are configured on the CronJob, Linux uses the daemon's zone, and EventBridge schedules use UTC unless a different schedule time zone is configured. Verify the scheduler's own DST behavior before relying on repeated or skipped local times.
